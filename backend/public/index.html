Fullstack E-Commerce API

The backend service powering the e-commerce application. It handles users, products, shopping carts, orders, and payments.

👤

Accounts

Create an account, sign in, and manage administrator access.

🛍️

Products

Browse products and manage the products available in the store.

🛒

Shopping Cart

Add products to your cart, view it, and update quantities.

📦

Orders

Place orders, view order history, and manage order status.

💳

Payments

Payment processing is supported through Stripe and Razorpay.

What can this API do?

  • Manage customer accounts and authentication
  • Provide products to the e-commerce frontend
  • Manage shopping carts for logged-in users
  • Create and manage customer orders
  • Process and verify online payments
